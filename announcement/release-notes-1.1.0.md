The Android app rebuilt from the ground up, in Material You by default, with the UwU Apps theme a switch away.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwuflash-1.1.0.apk` |

On an iPhone, a computer or any other phone, use [flash.uwuapps.org](https://flash.uwuapps.org) in the browser, and add it to your home screen to install it as an app.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**. 1.1.0 is a bigger download than before, about 60 MB, so you may want to be on Wi-Fi. Install it over the old one as usual: the first time it opens, your cards, pictures and settings come across by themselves, once, with nothing to do.

Your cards and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **A real Android app**: until now the app showed the website inside it. From 1.1.0 every screen is the app's own, built for Android, so it opens straight away, works with no internet from the very first open, and scrolls and animates smoothly.
- **Everything comes across**: the first time 1.1.0 opens, it brings over your cards, their pictures, your theme and your settings. A note at the bottom says so.
- **Material You by default**: the app follows your phone, in its own font and, on Android 12 and later, your wallpaper's colours, with solid surfaces, pill buttons and sheets that slide up from the bottom. Your cards take your wallpaper's colours too. Light or dark follows your phone until you pick one in **Theme**.
  - In **Theme**, a brand colour can stand in for your wallpaper's.
- **The UwU Apps theme**: turn **Material You** off in **Settings** or **Theme** for the look you know from the website, with its Jua font and glass surfaces. **Mode** and **Brand colour** work in both.
- **Settings**: a new gear button in the Edge Tray, with the **Material You** switch, **Theme**, **Notifications**, **Check for updates** and the version you're on.
- **The Edge Tray, smoother**: the same tray with the same buttons, sliding in and out and unrolling as the website's does.
- **The status bar is back**: the clock and your icons stay in view while you're editing. Presenting still hides every bar.
- **Everything else, as on the website**: the editor, layers and layouts, a picture filling the card with its crop, the ticker and wrapping, presenting with full brightness, the volume keys and flick, **Speak**, Mirror, Extend, Big Screen and Send cards, sharing with no wifi, notifications and links.
- **Sharing still works with everyone**: the website, and phones still on 1.0.0, share with 1.1.0 as before. Another phone on 1.1.0 shows your cards in your wallpaper's colours; the website and 1.0.0 show them in your brand colour.
