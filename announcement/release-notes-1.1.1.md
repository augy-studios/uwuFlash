## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwuflash-1.1.1.apk` |

On an iPhone, a computer or any other phone, use [flash.uwuapps.org](https://flash.uwuapps.org) in the browser, and add it to your home screen to install it as an app.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**. This update still comes through the old small window. Once you're on 1.1.1, the download-arrow button opens the new page.

Your cards and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **A new page for updates**: **Check for updates** now opens a full page, in your wallpaper's colours on Android 12 and later, light or dark as your phone is. It says what's happening, shows a download's progress on a wavy bar, and has large buttons at the bottom within easy reach.
- **What's new, on a timeline**: every version is on the page, marked **New** or **Installed**, with what changed in each under its heading. The one you'd get opens by itself, and any other opens with a tap.
- **Clearer when an update is stopped**: on a Samsung phone on Android 14 or later, when an update doesn't install, the page explains that Auto Blocker probably stopped it, opens Security settings for you, and carries on from there with **Try again**.
